## 最终提示词

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: original standalone logo symbol for "Dont Forget", a personal AI desktop assistant with long-term memory. The user explicitly wants the approximate silhouette of a small animal.
Primary request: design ONE exceptionally simple and distinctive little elephant logo. The elephant communicates remembering, gentle intelligence, and a reliable companion.
Subject and form: a compact friendly elephant head in a graceful three-quarter/profile view facing right, recognizable large rounded ear and a short upward-curled trunk. Build the entire mark from only a few clean, confident, chunky interlocking rounded shapes with generous negative space. The ear has one subtle folded-corner/bookmark-like cutout to suggest keeping an important thought. This must remain an elephant first, a quiet memory metaphor second. A single very small eye is enough. No extra props.
Style: premium contemporary software identity, flat vector-like bitmap artwork with extremely crisp edges, carefully balanced geometry and optical corrections. Calm, intelligent, friendly but not childish. A cohesive silhouette readable at 24px and usable as a desktop app symbol. Avoid looking like a detailed mascot illustration.
Color palette: monochrome deep forest teal green #286453, optional negative-space transparent cutouts only. Match a restrained neutral desktop workspace UI.
Composition: one centered isolated mark on a genuinely transparent background, square canvas, mark occupying approximately 75% of the width with generous equal padding. No surrounding rounded-square tile. No wordmark or lettering.
Constraints: one design only, no presentation board, no multiple variants, no mockups, no 3D, no gradients, no lighting, no shadows, no texture, no outline-heavy sketch, no tiny decorative details, no stars, no robot or brain motifs, no text, no watermark. The silhouette should feel original rather than imitate any known elephant brand. Preserve true background alpha.
