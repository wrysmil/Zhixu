## 最终提示词

Use case: illustration-story.
Asset type: tiny delightful mascot illustration perched on the upper-right edge of a personal AI desktop chat input, for a memory assistant called Dont Forget.
Primary request: ONE lively, beautifully crafted little elephant character, much more expressive and appealing than a flat logo. This is a polished product UI illustration intended to be displayed about 110 pixels wide.
Subject: a charming baby elephant in a slight three-quarter front view, rounded oversized head, two wide gently flared ears, bright kind eyes with subtle highlights, a cheerful relaxed smile, the head tilted with curiosity. Its two small front feet rest together on an INVISIBLE horizontal edge as if peeking over a chat box. One gently curled short trunk holds one small cream paper memo with a folded corner, a quiet remembering metaphor. Keep the trunk and both feet anatomically readable. Focus on face, ears and front feet, no full body, no visible lower body. Character facing slightly toward the left so it looks into the chat input.
Style: high-end contemporary soft 3D/clay editorial illustration, tactile smooth matte material, beautifully restrained rounded sculpting, subtle physically plausible volume and soft ambient self shading, a friendly desk companion. Cute and expressive without looking like cheap clip art, a toy photograph, or a corporate logo. Original character.
Palette: gentle sage/mint gray elephant body, warm soft peach inner ears, deep forest-green accents, one small ivory memo. Harmonize with white and neutral-gray desktop UI and deep green #286453. Well-defined light-colored face with clear medium-dark green contours and visible features against both white and charcoal UI backgrounds.
Composition: one isolated character, centered on a square canvas, head, ears, trunk and two small feet as a compact unified silhouette occupying roughly 82% of image width. Position feet at the bottom of the silhouette along one clean horizontal baseline to let a UI place it on the input border. Enough transparent padding so ears and trunk aren't clipped.
Background: genuinely transparent alpha.
Avoid: any text, letters, lettering, watermark, branding, decorative stars, sparkles, particles, orbiting objects, speech bubbles, border, background, ground shadow, visible table, visible ledge, rectangle, chat UI, landscape, presentation board, multiple characters, multiple variants, oversized props, photorealism. The tiny memo has NO writing. No robotic accessories or headphones.
